Privacy Policy

What we access from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the connected account's open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to upload approved clips to the account's inbox as drafts.
  • video.list: to read the account's recent videos and record the link of a posted clip.

Storage

Access and refresh tokens and post links are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

Disconnect ClipDesk Studio in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) to revoke access. Stored tokens are deleted on request or when the account is removed from the tool. To ask for deletion, email the contact below.
