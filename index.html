ClipDesk

ClipDesk is a desktop tool for one creator. It cuts short vertical clips from gaming streams the creator has the right to use, lets the creator review each clip, and sends approved clips to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. ClipDesk prepares short clips and shows each one to the creator for approval.
  3. An approved clip is uploaded to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, video.upload).
  4. The creator opens the TikTok app, edits the caption if they want, and posts it themselves.
  5. ClipDesk reads the creator's latest videos (video.list) to record the link of the post.

ClipDesk is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner on their own accounts.
